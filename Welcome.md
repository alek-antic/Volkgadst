Hello @Mythic Bastionland!

You might be wondering why there are so many of you in this chat when there are only 5 players at the table at a time! In the spirit of experimenting, I wanted to attempt something new for scheduling this month.

In Mythic Bastionland, every session ends with the passage of time. Weeks, a Season, or an Age may pass between when we see knights active in the realm of Volkgadst. This means that the realm will truly have a rotating cast of characters across the four sessions, with the Myth being the story across the ages that comes from the knights' journeys.

You will see in #mythic-roster four calendar events for the month of May. Before RSVPing, consider leaving space for everyone to join in on the communal experiment! I would recommend starting out by signing up for 2 calendar events, and then a third one after a few days if there are some events without 5 players signed up.

Additionally, there will be no session 0; I would appreciate everyone showing up to their sessions with a filled-out character sheet. In a few days (when I have time) I will provide more detailed instructions here. The game's rules and a character sheet PDF will be available in #game-reference.
