#### Bursten forth out shroud o' night
#### Graven wheel and tiger's bite

## Omens
1. The stars are visible, even in day, forming a wheel pattern, visibly turning.
2. A huge crater, barely the rubble of a stone outpost left. Heavy footprints within.
3. A distraught family claim a pair of great tigers attacked their small farm, eating their chickens, sheep, and dog. They claim the beasts fell from the sky and vanished back into the darkness.
4. A swathe has been cut through a forest or mountain. It leads toward the Myth Hex (0904)
5. The Charioteer rides over the horizon, pulled by two great tigers. He is quick to share his past triumphs, gathering followers to feed his ego. He seeks revenge against the ruler of the nearest Holding, who has slighted him in a minor way.
6. Following up on a boast that somebody doubted, the Charioteer is preparing to charge either upstream, or into the tide, proving that no mere water is a match for the Chariot. They will succeed at this, driving the water away for good, before taking flight and returning to the stars.
